Investigation

Turn raw intelligence into decisions your team can act on

LunarChain connects cyber threats, geopolitical activity, military reporting, organizations, locations, vendors, people of interest, and exposed assets into one intelligence graph. Clients can monitor conflict zones around areas of interest, investigate IOCs tied to recent cyber campaigns, and understand which actors, weapons, infrastructure, or suppliers are connected to the events they care about.

Investigation Outcomes

Monitor areas of interest

Track conflict zones, border regions, facilities, trade routes, or operating areas and see related incidents, local reporting, associated threat actors, military units, and geopolitical context as it changes.

Investigate cyber campaigns

Start from an IOC, malware family, domain, IP address, or article connected to a recent campaign and pivot to related infrastructure, threat actors, targets, sectors, and prior reporting.

Understand people, groups, and weaponry

Follow named people, organizations, armed groups, weapons systems, equipment, and incidents in regional reporting, including Middle East conflict coverage and other areas of concern.

Brief risk to the business

Connect intelligence to your vendors, facilities, executives, brands, and operating footprint so teams can prioritize exposed assets and explain why an event matters.
