Custom Intelligence Operations

Tailored intelligence operations for niche environments

LunarSurface can be configured around the domains you monitor, the sources you trust, the entities you track, and the decisions your team needs to make.

01

Cyber Threat Monitoring And Incident Enrichment

A custom monitoring workspace for security teams that tracks threat activity around your organization, sector, vendors, exposed assets, and priority technologies.

Monitor threat actors, malware reporting, phishing infrastructure, leaked credentials, vulnerability exploitation, suspicious domains, and IOCs so analysts can investigate faster and prioritize what matters.

02

Defence Contract And Delivery Risk Monitoring

For defence manufacturers, suppliers, and project teams managing high-value programmes across sensitive regions, customers, and delivery routes.

Monitor the customer, end user, operating region, supplier network, shipping lanes, sanctions exposure, conflict activity, border incidents, infrastructure disruption, and public reporting around a named programme.

Help commercial, compliance, and operations teams understand what could affect bid decisions, delivery timelines, site access, export reviews, or mission planning.

03

Executive Protection And Convoy Route Intelligence

For private security operators, corporate security teams, and armed response providers protecting executives, VIPs, residences, offices, venues, and planned movements.

Build route and residence intelligence around the principal, convoy route, destination, and surrounding area. Monitor crime reports, protests, road closures, unrest, weather, medical access, incidents near venues, and local reporting before and during movement.

Use alerts and briefings to support control rooms, drivers, close protection teams, and response units with timely context for go, delay, reroute, or escalation decisions.

04

Humanitarian Crisis And Access Monitoring

For NGOs and aid organizations operating in unstable regions. Track conflict activity, displacement, border closures, infrastructure damage, disease outbreaks, sanctions, and access risks that could affect field teams, aid delivery, or reporting.

05

Anti-Corruption And Procurement Risk Monitoring

For investigative teams, compliance groups, and anti-corruption organizations. Monitor companies, officials, contracts, sanctions, court records, adverse media, ownership links, and regional political risk around a case, country, or procurement process.

06

Wildlife Trafficking And Environmental Crime Monitoring

For conservation groups and enforcement partners. Track seizure reports, trafficking routes, ports, border crossings, species mentions, suspected networks, online marketplaces, and regional enforcement activity.
