SafeRoute privacy policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated 14 October 2026

This policy covers the SafeRoute mobile app published by Michael Sykes under the LunarChain name. This release offers public route planning and guidance without accounts, convoy planning, advertising or advertising tracking. Contact info@lunarchain.net with privacy questions or requests.

Information used to provide the app

  • Locations and routes: with your permission, the app uses your device location to show your position and guide an active trip. Searches, selected locations, route coordinates and the map region are sent to our service to find places, calculate routes, obtain road information and check risk coverage. Recalculating a route can send your current position again.
  • Searches: location search text and nearby coordinates are processed and cached to return relevant results.
  • Technical information: our hosting and network services process IP addresses, request information, errors and performance measurements to deliver the service, diagnose faults, limit abuse and keep it secure.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send. Do not send passwords or information unnecessary to your request.

Information on your device

Saved places, recent destinations, route preferences, cached risk information and active-guidance state are stored on your device. Background guidance stores the latest usable location sample locally; it is not a continuous location-sharing feature. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings.

Services that process information

Our service uses Hetzner hosting and Cloudflare networking. Apple Maps supplies the iOS map. OpenStreetMap-based services, including Photon place search, OSRM routing and Overpass road/facility queries, and Open-Elevation terrain queries may receive the search text or geographic information needed for the requested feature. Opening this website uses Firebase Hosting. Opening a risk-source link takes you to that publisher's website.

These services are not used by SafeRoute for advertising or cross-app tracking. Their processing is governed by their applicable privacy policies and service terms: Apple, Hetzner, Cloudflare, komoot / Photon, OpenStreetMap Foundation and Google / Firebase. These policies do not necessarily cover every independently operated OpenStreetMap-based service.

Retention

On-device saved places and preferences remain until you remove them or delete the app. Active-guidance state is cleared when you end a trip; cached information is replaced or expires as the app operates. Server search and route-enrichment caches are bounded and reused until expiry or eviction. Geographic research regions and the resulting public risk catalogue can be retained to maintain shared coverage; they are not a named user's trip history.

Operational and security records are retained for service operation, troubleshooting, abuse prevention and any applicable legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained while needed to handle your request and follow-up. You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting us; we may need enough information to identify the relevant records and may retain records where required for security or legal reasons.

Your choices

Change or revoke location access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → SafeRoute. Background access is optional and used only during an authorized active trip. You can still search and plan from manually selected locations without granting device-location access; live guidance requires it. Remove saved and recent places in the planner. Deleting the app removes its local app-container data; separately manage any device backups. Ending guidance stops its background location task.

We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. Information may be processed outside your country by the services described above. If you have a privacy concern or wish to exercise applicable data-protection rights, email info@lunarchain.net. Depending on your location, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
