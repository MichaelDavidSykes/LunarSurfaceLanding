SafeRoute support

For help, feedback, inaccurate risk information or a privacy request, email info@lunarchain.net. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Share screenshots or route details only if you are comfortable doing so; please remove private information first.

Planning a route

Search for a destination, edit the starting location or add stops, then plot the route. Hold and drag stops to reorder them. Expand the route card to compare available alternatives. Cancel returns to the planner; Start begins guidance and End finishes it. Meaningful alternatives are not available for every journey.

Location and connectivity

Internet access is needed to search, calculate routes and update risk coverage. Device location is optional for manually selected route planning, but required for live guidance. Review its permissions in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → SafeRoute. Background permission lets an active trip continue while the screen is locked.

Understanding risk information

Tap a risk area or route alert to read its details and available source links. A lack of mapped risks does not mean an area is safe. Routes prioritize mapped risk avoidance where practical, but may cross risk areas when alternatives are unavailable or impractical. Review remaining-risk notices and local conditions before travelling.

SafeRoute does not guarantee personal safety, provide emergency response or replace road signs, closures or local advice. Do not interact with the app while driving. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

Accounts and payments

Version 1.0.0 does not require sign-in and does not offer in-app purchases or convoy accounts.
